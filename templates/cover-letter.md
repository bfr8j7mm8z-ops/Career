# Cover letter template

Copy to `cover-letters/template.md`. Structure only; each letter is written fresh per application.

1. Why this company/role specifically (one concrete, researched detail, not generic enthusiasm)
2. Two or three of your achievements mapped directly to what the role asks for
3. Close: availability / notice period, and what you'd like to talk about

Under 300 words. Plain voice. No "I am writing to express my interest".
