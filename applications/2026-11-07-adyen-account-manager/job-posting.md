## The role (from search summaries)
Manage relationships with innovative international merchants across multiple industries and channels. Work
directly with strategic merchants, build long-term partnerships to reduce churn, collaborate with the team and
resolve issues early. Adyen's offering spans e-commerce, in-person and embedded payments and financial services.
The 27 Oct review noted the posting asks for roughly 3-6 years client-facing commercial experience.

## Process (from the 27 Oct review)
Recruiter screen, team interview, case study, leadership interview, final with a senior executive.
