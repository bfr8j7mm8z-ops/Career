## What is your motivation to join Adyen?
Adyen helps international merchants get paid however and wherever their customers want to pay, and I want my career to be in payments. I grew up in Kenya watching M-Pesa change how a whole country paid, and I've since worked with international retail brands, built a marketplace's split-payment flow at SalesWolf, and found an embedded-finance opportunity at Jeri. I see the global opportunity in payments and fintech, and Adyen is where I'd learn it from the inside, working directly with strategic merchants.

## Why do you believe that you are a good fit for this role?
This role is about growing and keeping strategic merchants, and I already do that. I manage $1.24M ARR across international retail and e-commerce brands, closed $216K ARR of renewals within six weeks of starting, saved an account that had served notice, and led the relationship as Dyson grew 4–5× across four markets. So I know the merchant's side of the table and how to keep a relationship growing. I'm an engineer, comfortable with technical teams, and I learn fast: I closed our largest marketing mix modelling contract within weeks of the product's launch. I haven't worked in payments yet, and I'd bring the same drive to learning Adyen's.
