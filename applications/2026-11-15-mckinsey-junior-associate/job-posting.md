## The role
Join a client service team and take ownership of a workstream on clients' toughest challenges (Fortune 500
to start-ups). Uncover the true challenges behind a client's strategy and create actionable plans. Combine
research, data analysis, interviews and brainstorming to generate insights. Work directly with clients to
solve problems, refine strategies and implement transformations, and leave them able to keep improving.
Judgement and integrity matter. High performance culture: resilience, feedback, apprenticeship.

## Qualifications and skills
- Degree requirements vary by country or practice.
- Excellent organisation: initiate tasks independently and see them through.
- Strong written and verbal communication, adjusting style to different perspectives and seniority levels.
- Rational decision making based on data, facts and logical reasoning.
- Able to create work products: PowerPoint decks, Excel models, written deliverables.
- Exceptional time management in a complex, largely autonomous environment.
- Able to work outside traditional hours or at short notice, and to travel to varied client environments.
