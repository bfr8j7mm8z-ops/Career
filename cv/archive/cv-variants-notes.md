## Patterns worth keeping
- **Summary** reframes by target: fintech/partnerships (GC), investment (PE/Inv), retention/portfolio (CSAM).
- **Headline numbers move up** to the first bullet or summary line when they match the role (retention % for CS, finance outputs for investment).
- **Fospha bullets** split into: retention/commercial, partnerships/cross-functional, analytical, automation/AI. Reorder these by role.
- **SalesWolf** can be pitched as GTM/partnerships (GC), unit economics and fundraising (PE), or payments product (fintech).
- **Jeri** is full detail for investment roles, one line for CS roles.
- **Schneider** only for investment/ESG roles.
- **Interests** swapped by target (Kenya/MPesa/fintech for payments roles; green tech/impact for investing).
