# CV format (current standard)

The CV Harry submitted to PPRO on 5 Nov 2026 (Account Executive, EMEA) is the format every new CV starts
from: `applications/2026-10-31-ppro-account-executive-emea/cv.md`. It replaces the June 2026 GoCardless
CSAM CV as the master layout. Facts still come only from the master profile.

## Page
- One A4 page, rendered by `automations/docs/render.py` (GoCardless styling: Calibri, blue name and
  headings with a rule, 10pt body, right-aligned dates). Check the page count after every change.
- Sections in order: PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY, EXPERIENCE, EDUCATION, SKILLS & TOOLS.

## Summary (Harry's own structure, about 5 lines)
1. Role and book: "Enterprise Account Manager at a fast-paced SaaS scale-up, owning a $1.24M ARR book
   across multi-market European brands."
2. How he works: C-suite relationships, working with internal teams (CEO and CCO, product) to grow accounts.
3. Results and style: strong growth (and retention where the role is about current business), "clear
   communicator and collaborator", partners (Google, Pinterest) and the product team driving customer value.
4. Drive: "Driven and proactive – co-founded a B2B marketplace in Brazil with an automated split-payment
   flow, signing its first 8 retail partners from zero."
5. Background: South America, Europe and Africa, and M-Pesa in Kenya.
Tailor only sentences 2 and 3 to the role (5–10% of the CV). Never scale him down for a junior role.

## Skills & Tools
- Core: rewritten for every posting in its own terms, pushing the limit (labels only).
- Tools: HubSpot, Amplitude, Jira, Notion, Claude Code, Excel & PowerPoint (advanced), Microsoft Suite.
- Languages with CEFR levels. Interests end with the Kenya / African fintech and payments (M-Pesa) line.

## Words
- "AI automation workflows", not "Claude skills".
- No semicolons, no ≈ or ~ before numbers, no colon joining two halves of a bullet, mixed bullet lengths,
  no first person.
