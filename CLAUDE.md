# Career repo

Purpose: long-term career management — CVs, cover letters, job applications, and job-search automation.

## Conventions

- Keep one folder per job application under `applications/`, named `YYYY-MM-DD-company-role/`, containing the job posting, tailored CV/cover letter, and notes.
- Track application status in `job-search-log.md`.
- Treat all content here as personal and private. Client brand names, stakeholder names/titles and commercial context (pricing, MRR, deal notes) are OK as Harry's own working notes. Not OK: third parties' personal data beyond name/title, or colleagues' HR/health details — refer to colleagues by role.
- `profile/master-profile.md` is the evidence base for all CVs, cover letters and interview prep — draw from it, and keep its ✅/⚠️ verification flags and "led / pod-led / supported" credit labels honest.

## Notes for future sessions

- No build/test tooling yet — this is a documents/automation workspace, not a codebase in the traditional sense.
- When adding automations (job search, CV updates, application drafting), document how to run them in `automations/README.md`.
