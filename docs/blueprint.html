Job Hunt Blueprint

A phone-friendly app where each job hunt is its own project. You describe a project in plain English, set the filters, and press Run. Claude searches, cleans and scores the results, and new matches arrive as notifications. You swipe through them, and for the ones you like Claude researches the company and writes a CV and cover letter that go through a tough review loop. It then helps you apply and tracks every application. A weekly review keeps improving the whole thing.

The app is live: open Job Hunt. Version 2 folds in your answers and your seven comments. Changes are marked new in v2.

How it fits together new in v2

Three parts. You only ever touch the first.
What you use

The app

A private page on claude.ai. It opens on your phone in the Claude app or a browser, with no install or hosting.

  • Projects in a sidebar
  • Filters as toggles
  • Jobs feed, Applications, Profile, Insights
  • Uploads for CVs and cover letters
Where it's stored

The app's database

Built into the page. Only you can open it, and Claude can read and write it.

  • Projects and their filters
  • Jobs, scores, decisions
  • Applications and next steps
  • Your uploads and profile
What does the work

Claude runs

A Claude session in the cloud with this repo: the skill, the tested engine, your Reed key and your connectors.

  • Started by Run, or nightly at 8pm
  • One run per project, in parallel
  • Writes results back to the app
  • Sends a push notification when done
Run button → starts a cloud run for that project 8pm schedule → one run per active project Run → Indeed · Reed · career pages · Gmail alerts → app database Run finished → push to your phone
Where things live. Your instinct was right: the repo is the backend. It holds the engine code, Claude's instructions, default settings and the data contract (app/SCHEMA.md). The app is the interface, plus a small built-in database for live records such as projects, jobs, decisions and uploads. That database exists because a claude.ai page can't read a private GitHub repo directly, and it lets the app update instantly on your phone. Nothing is lost if the page changes: it is republished from the repo file and keeps its data.

At your computer with Chrome on, Claude opens the application, fills it from your approved CV, letter and profile, then stops for you to check and press Submit. CV and cover letter gauntlet new in v2

Every draft must survive a panel of critics before you see it. Up to three rounds, then whatever is unresolved is flagged for you.
STAGE 1

Decode the job

  • Must-haves vs nice-to-haves
  • The real problem the hire solves
  • Language the posting uses
  • Company intel worth using
STAGE 2

Evidence map

  • Each requirement matched to proof from your uploads
  • Gaps listed honestly
  • The strongest 3 stories picked
STAGE 3

Draft

  • CV trimmed and reordered from the master
  • Letter in your voice, from your letters
  • One concrete company hook
STAGE 4

The gauntlet

  • Six critics review it (below)
  • Any fail sends it back for a rewrite
  • Round limit: 3
STAGE 5

Your review

  • Scorecard per critic
  • What changed and why
  • Your edits feed your voice notes
Truth checkerCan every claim, number and title be traced to your uploads? Anything that can't is removed. This one cannot be overruled.
6-second recruiterIn a skim, is it obvious you match the top three must-haves?
Hiring managerWould this person solve my problem? Are the stories specific to the role?
ATS readerDoes the CV cover the posting's key terms where true, and does it parse cleanly with a single column and standard headings?
Voice and humaniserDoes it sound like your past cover letters, not AI? No stock phrases or uniform bullet rhythm.
Red teamWhat is the strongest reason to reject this application, and has the draft dealt with it?
Your existing skills for job ads, CV writing, CV review, cover letters and humanising each slot into a stage, so we reuse them rather than starting from scratch.

How it learns who you are this is what makes CVs sound like you over time

Bullet library

Every line you approve is saved to your library, with the role, the skill it proves and the evidence behind it. New CVs start from your approved wording, not from scratch. When you edit a tailored CV, you're asked whether to update the library too.

Style rules

Claude turns your edits into plain rules you can read and change in Profile, for example "Says 'I led', never 'spearheaded'", "Keeps numbers exact", "Letters open with the company, not me". Every draft and the voice critic follow them.

Story bank

Your best achievements written up once as short stories: situation, what you did, result, and proof. They feed CV bullets, cover letters and interview prep.
